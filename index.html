About Date Spark

Date Spark gives you and your partner a steady supply of conversation starters — fun icebreakers, deep questions, and everything in between — so date night never runs dry.

Browse by category, like the ones you enjoy, and save your favorites to a running list to ask your spouse. Everything is stored right in this browser, so nothing leaves your device.

Want to share your saved list with your partner's device? Use Export to download a text file, then have them use Import to add it to their list.
